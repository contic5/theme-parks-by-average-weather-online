Methodology

The heat and humidity values are based on averaged values for each month over 10 years (around 300 dates are averaged for each month). The heat is by day. The humidity is calculated by hour and converted into daily values. The daily minimum humidity is the minimum hourly humidity, the daily average humidity is the average of the 24 hourly humidity hours, and the maximum humidity is the maximum hourly humidity. This is how the selectable options are defined

  • Minimum Heat: The average of the daily low temperature
  • Average Heat: The average of the daily average temperature.
  • Maximum Heat: The average of the daily high temperature.
  • Minimum Humidity: The average of the daily minimum humidity
  • Average Humidity: The average of the daily average humidity.
  • Maximum Humidity: The average of the daily maximum humidity.
